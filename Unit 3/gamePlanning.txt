1: The game is a one time-combat simulator where you post something super absurd on accident and the
entire fandom of of social media finds where you live, doxes you and comes to try and harm you, but
you have weapons to defend yourself.
My mechanics will be a boost engine, the player would have a boost speed to run faster, and will also have a combat engine
that can punch, kick, slap, etc. And will have weapons to fight off enemies and NPCS. The game will be a combat while your player
has to run away from angry crowds after posting something super absurd and have to fight to the death. 

2: The controls are simple, You can use your phone , or will be keyboard and mouse.
You use WASD keys to move left right,up and down. The space bar can be used for jumping, but if you have a weapon,
you can hold space to use a critical hit. The shift button will be used for running,the Q key is to pick upan object,
the E key is to critical hit with, your fists and the RMB is to aim with your gun.

The objects that will be in the game would be combat knifes, guns, crowbars, bats, any that can save and protect yourself.
You would have only one life since the game runs on realistic memory that'll remember your actions, any mistake
logged will sort in the file, causing an outcome. To prevent permenatly dying, that is why you have weapons
to defend yourself and to protect your life. The knife does 20-40 damage depending on the charge. (light hit,20 damage
critical hit: 40 damage) the gun can do loads of damage. (Head shit: 100 damage = complete kill, stomach shot,
50 damage, chest shot: 60 damage, leg/arm damage: 30-45 damage) The crowbar and bat work the same. (Head hit: 90-100 damage
if swung critical. If hit light,70-80 damage, anywhere else on the body does 30-70 damage.)
